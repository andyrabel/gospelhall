Privacy & Data Notice

What this site does

Gospel Hall Directory displays publicly listed contact information for Brethren assemblies in England, Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales, and the Republic of Ireland. The data is fetched live each time you visit and is not stored by this website.

What personal data is shown

Assembly records may include a meeting name, address, postcode, telephone number, email address, and website. Some of these details may be personal data where they refer to an individual (for example, a personal mobile number or personal email address).

Lawful basis

Data is processed on the basis of legitimate interest — providing a publicly accessible directory of places of worship to help people find their local assembly. This site does not collect, sell, or share any visitor data, and does not use cookies or tracking technologies.
