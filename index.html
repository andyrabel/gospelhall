A Directory of Churches

with their roots in the Plymouth Brethren movement

Gospel Halls (sometimes called Plymouth Brethren assemblies) are informal gatherings of Christians who meet simply, without a paid clergy or formal denomination, seeking to follow the New Testament pattern of worship and fellowship. Most assemblies hold a Breaking of Bread service on Sunday mornings, open to all who know the Lord Jesus as Saviour, and gospel meetings welcoming everyone. Some appreciate visitors bringing a letter of commendation from their home assembly, though all are warmly received.

Data is fetched live from published Google Sheets. Contact individual assemblies for the most up-to-date information.

Privacy & Data Notice

What this site does

Gospel Hall Directory displays publicly listed contact information for Brethren assemblies in England, Northern Ireland, Scotland, Wales, and the Republic of Ireland. The data is fetched live each time you visit and is not stored by this website.

What personal data is shown

Assembly records may include a meeting name, address, postcode, telephone number, email address, and website. Some of these details may be personal data where they refer to an individual (for example, a personal mobile number or personal email address).

Mapping & geocoding

Assembly postcodes and locations are sent to postcodes.io (UK) and Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (Ireland) to obtain map co-ordinates. Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap. Results are cached in your browser's local storage to avoid repeat requests. No personal visitor data is transmitted.

Lawful basis

Data is processed on the basis of legitimate interest — providing a publicly accessible directory of places of worship to help people find their local assembly. This site does not collect, sell, or share any visitor data, and does not use cookies or tracking technologies.
